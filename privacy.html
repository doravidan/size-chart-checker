Privacy

Short version: the size matching runs in your browser. Your measurements and the size charts you paste are never sent to us or anyone else.

  • Measurements: if you tick "Remember my measurements on this device", they are saved in your browser's local storage on this device only. Untick it or press "Forget" to delete them.
  • Analytics: if analytics are switched on for this site, we count anonymous page visits and clicks on buttons (for example "Get Style My Look on the App Store") with a cookieless analytics service. These counts never include your measurements or charts.
  • App Store link: the App Store link carries a campaign label (for example ct=sizechk-pinterest) so we can see roughly which channel sent visitors. Apple shows us only totals.
  • No accounts, no email, no cookies set by this page.

This tool is made by the team behind Style My Look, an iPhone virtual try-on app. Style My Look has its own privacy policy, available in the app and on aitryon.studio.
