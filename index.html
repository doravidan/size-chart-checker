How to measure (1 minute)
  • Bust/chest: around the fullest part, tape level under the arms.
  • Waist: around the narrowest part of your torso, usually just above the belly button.
  • Hips: around the fullest part of your hips and seat, feet together.
  • Keep the tape snug but not tight, and breathe out normally.

Questions

How accurate is this?

It is exactly as good as the chart you paste and the measurements you enter. It does the comparison carefully and tells you when your measurements disagree, but brands cut differently, so read the fabric and the reviews too.

My measurements point to two sizes. Which one?

The checker leans to the size that isn't tight on the key measurements for that item (bust for tops, waist and hips for bottoms, all three for dresses), because a tailor can take clothes in but can rarely let them out. For stretchy fabric it allows a closer fit.

What's the difference between body and garment charts?

A body chart says who the size is meant for ("bust 88-92 cm"). A garment chart measures the clothes, which are bigger than your body by design. For garment charts the checker adds normal wearing room (rule-of-thumb amounts, adjusted by your fit preference).

Do you store my measurements?

No. The size matching runs entirely in this page, and your measurements and charts are never sent anywhere. If "remember" is ticked, your numbers are kept only in this browser's local storage, and "Forget" deletes them. Privacy.
